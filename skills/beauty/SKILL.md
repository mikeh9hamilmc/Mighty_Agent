# beauty

The beauty skill provides access to an autonomous beauty consultant and research assistant.

## Capabilities

- **Routine Analysis**: Read through personal routines and products in `skills/beauty/data/`.
- **Beauty Research**: Search the web for skincare products, treatments, and guidelines.
- **Consultation**: Provide advice on anti-aging and makeup based on your documents.
- **Memory**: Remembers preferences, routines, and skin history across sessions.

## Usage

You can invoke this skill by asking a beauty question directly, or by prefixing your message with "ask beauty: ".

**Example:**
"ask beauty: what is a good morning routine for dry skin?"
"ask beauty: read my current products and tell me if they conflict"
