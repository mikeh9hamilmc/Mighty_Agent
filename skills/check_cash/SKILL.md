# IB Account Cash Checker

This skill connects to your Interactive Brokers account (TWS or Gateway) and retrieves key financial metrics.

## Features
- **Cash Balance**: Shows available USD cash.
- **Net Liquidation**: Total value of cash + positions.
- **Buying Power**: Remaining purchasing capacity.
- **Cash Percentage**: Calculates what percentage of your total account is currently held in cash.

## Usage
"How much cash do I have in IB?"
"Check my account buying power"
"What is my net liquidation value?"
