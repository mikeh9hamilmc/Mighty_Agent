# UPRO Position Checker

This skill specifically monitors your UPRO holdings in Interactive Brokers.

## Features
- **Share Count**: Displays the total number of UPRO shares currently in your portfolio.
- **Average Price**: Shows the average cost basis for your UPRO position.

## Usage
"How many UPRO shares do I have?"
"What is my average price for UPRO?"
"Check my UPRO position"
